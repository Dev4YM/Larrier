# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Larrier to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their
experience, background or identity. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull
requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented) agrees to
make that true.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that people disagree, and decide on the merits.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic noise.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that don't follow this code, and may ban anyone from the
project, temporarily or permanently, for behavior they find harmful.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Dev4YM). Every report is reviewed and kept
confidential.
